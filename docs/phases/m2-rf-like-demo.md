# Milestone M2 — RF-like demo

**Goal:** a public demo that proves Greenrune is an RF-like: farming, combat, taming, forging and a dungeon feeding each other.

**M2 is complete when:**
- The demo is downloadable, covers Spring and Summer with Dungeon 1 and Boss 1, and ends on an end card on Summer 30.
- It holds a steady 60 fps on the low-end target, both in town on festival day and in the field with 10 monsters.
- At least 3 of 5 testers rate farming and combat within 1 point of each other on a 1–5 scale *(default)*.
- Feedback is triaged into a list, with feel fixes ranked first.

**Starts when:** [Phase 7](phase-07-dungeons.md) is done.

**Steps:**
1. **Demo cut.** Limit the demo to Spring and Summer with Dungeon 1 and Boss 1; an end card on Summer 30; demo saves stay compatible with the full game. Make Dungeon 1 reachable within the first in-game week *(default)*: RF4 opens its first dungeon on day 2 ([RF4 and Farnia research › Milestones](../rf4-farnia-research.md#milestones)).
2. **Performance.** Pick a low-end target machine (e.g. a laptop with integrated graphics); profile the town on festival day and the field with 10 monsters; fix the top costs (draw calls, lights, AI updates).
3. **Feel playtest.** 5 testers play the demo and rate farming and combat 1–5; ask each "Which felt worse, farming or combat?"; fix feel issues before any new content.
4. **Public page.** A store page (itch.io, or Steam with a Steamworks account); a short trailer or GIFs showing the farm → fight → tame → craft loop; a feedback channel (comments or a form).

**Checklist:**
- [ ] A new demo game reaches the end card on Summer 30
- [ ] A new demo game reaches Dungeon 1 within the first in-game week
- [ ] A demo save loads in the full game
- [ ] The profiler shows a steady 60 fps on the target in both scenes
- [ ] The public page shows the farm → fight → tame → craft loop and links a feedback channel

**Out of scope for this milestone:** new systems or content beyond the demo cut.

**Reference:** RF4's first dungeon on day 2 ([RF4 and Farnia research › Milestones](../rf4-farnia-research.md#milestones)).
