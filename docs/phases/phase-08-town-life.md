# Phase 8 — Town life depth

**Goal:** a fuller town with requests, more festivals, romance and marriage. The town feels busier every season.
**Estimate:** 4–6 months (~17–26 weeks). Writing-heavy.
**Starts when:** [M2](m2-rf-like-demo.md) is done.
**Background:** [research › Requests](../rf4-research.md#town-management-orders-prince-points-and-requests), [research › Festivals](../rf4-research.md#festivals), [research › relationships](../rf4-research.md#npc-relationships-dating-marriage-and-children).

Every checked item must meet the [definition of done](../endgame.md#definition-of-done).

## Step 1 — More NPCs (weeks 1–6)

- [ ] 5–7 new NPCs, bringing the town to 10–12
- [ ] Each one: schedule, tastes, birthday, ~50 lines
- [ ] Interiors or spots for each new NPC

**Done when:** 10–12 NPCs walk their routines for a full season with no stuck or vanished NPCs.

## Step 2 — Requests (weeks 7–10)

- [ ] `RequestDef` data: type (ship, give, defeat, craft), target, count, reward, unlock condition
- [ ] Request board with tracking in the HUD
- [ ] ~30 board requests written; rewards are seeds, recipes and tools
- [ ] Daily repeatable requests drawn from a pool
- [ ] Personal requests from villagers (+ friendship)

**Done when:** every one of the ~30 board requests can be completed in a test run.

## Step 3 — Festivals to 8 (weeks 11–14)

- [ ] 6 new festivals, 2 per season in total, each unlocked by an Order
- [ ] At most 2 minigame festivals; the rest are exhibitions (cooking, monster, crop)
- [ ] Every festival recurs yearly once ordered

**Done when:** every season has 2 festivals, and each one runs and pays out.

## Step 4 — Romance (weeks 15–20)

- [ ] 2–4 marriage candidates chosen from the NPCs
- [ ] Confession at a high friendship level → lover state
- [ ] Dates: invite, meet at a place and time, short scene; 3 dates needed
- [ ] ~3 events per candidate
- [ ] Rejection and jealousy rules kept simple (one lover at a time)

**Done when:** each candidate can be dated through all their events in a test run.

## Step 5 — Marriage (weeks 21–23)

- [ ] Ring recipe at the crafting table
- [ ] Bigger bed through an Order
- [ ] Proposal and wedding scene
- [ ] Spouse moves to the farmhouse with a new schedule and lines

**Done when:** after the wedding, the spouse lives on the farm and says spouse lines.

## Step 6 — Skills and diary (weeks 24–25)

- [ ] Skills to ~12 total
- [ ] Bedside diary listing active events and requests

**Done when:** the diary shows every active request and the next romance step.

## Step 7 — Save v9 and playtest (week 26)

- [ ] Save v9 adds requests, romance and marriage state; v8 saves still load
- [ ] Marriage playthrough from a new game

**Done when:** a v8 save loads into v9, and the marriage run has no bugs left open.

## Phase done when

- [ ] Married one candidate in a full playthrough
- [ ] ~30 board requests all completable
- [ ] 2 festivals per season
- [ ] Every NPC has at least one season-specific line per season
- [ ] Requests and relationships survive save → quit → load

## Not in this phase

Children, more candidates, story arc.
