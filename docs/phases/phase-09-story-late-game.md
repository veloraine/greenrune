# Phase 9 — Story & late game

**Goal:** one short optional story arc with an ending, plus reasons to keep playing after the credits (RF4's loop weakens after its credits; this fixes that).
**Estimate:** 4–6 months (~17–26 weeks).
**Starts when:** [Phase 8](phase-08-town-life.md) is done.
**Background:** [research › Story structure](../rf4-research.md#story-structure), [research › Crafting](../rf4-research.md#crafting-forging-cooking-chemistry-and-upgrades).

Every checked item must meet the [definition of done](../endgame.md#definition-of-done).

## Step 1 — Story outline (weeks 1–2)

- [ ] One-page arc outline: ~5 beats, a villain, an ending
- [ ] Each beat tied to an existing system (a dungeon, a festival, a villager)
- [ ] Rule written at the top: the story never blocks farming or town life

**Done when:** the outline fits on one page and every beat names the system it uses.

## Step 2 — Dungeon 3 and story boss (weeks 3–10)

- [ ] Dungeon 3, ~20 minutes, built on the Phase 7 framework
- [ ] Story boss with the Phase 7 boss rules
- [ ] After defeat, the boss joins the town as an NPC (schedule, tastes, lines)

**Done when:** beating the boss adds a new villager the next day.

## Step 3 — Story events and ending (weeks 11–14)

- [ ] 4–5 story events in-engine (camera moves + Dialogic)
- [ ] Every event has a guaranteed trigger, never a random one
- [ ] Ending scene and credits

**Done when:** the story can be finished from a new game, and no event depends on chance.

## Step 4 — Post-story dungeon (weeks 15–18)

- [ ] Unlocks after the credits
- [ ] Hardest monsters and best materials

**Done when:** it can't be cleared in Dungeon 2 gear and can be cleared in its own crafted gear.

## Step 5 — Late-game crafting ceiling (weeks 19–20)

- [ ] 2x and 8x multiplier materials (repeat the previous upgrade's effect)
- [ ] One stat-inverting material (later penalties become bonuses)
- [ ] All three found only in the post-story dungeon

**Done when:** a min-maxed weapon is far stronger than intended, and that's allowed.

## Step 6 — Late-game farming and modes (weeks 21–23)

- [ ] Level-10 seeds reachable through reaping within ~2 in-game years
- [ ] Contest scoring rewards high crop levels
- [ ] Hard difficulty
- [ ] New Game+ after all board requests are done (decide and write down what carries over)

**Done when:** level-10 seeds are reached in a test save, and New Game+ starts with the agreed carry-over.

## Step 7 — Post-credits playtest (weeks 24–26)

- [ ] A tester finishes the story, then keeps playing
- [ ] Note what they do after the credits and why they stop

**Done when:** the tester plays at least one more in-game season after the credits by choice.

## Phase done when

- [ ] The story can be finished, and ignoring it never blocks anything else
- [ ] No story event depends on a random trigger
- [ ] Post-story dungeon, crafting ceiling, level-10 seeds and New Game+ all work
- [ ] A tester keeps playing after the credits by choice
- [ ] Story progress survives save → quit → load

## Not in this phase

More arcs, anime cutscenes, children, magic.
