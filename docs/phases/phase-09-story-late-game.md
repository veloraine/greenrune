# Phase 9 — Story & late game

**Goal:** one short optional story arc with an ending, plus reasons to keep playing after the credits (RF4's loop weakens after its credits; this fixes that).

**Phase 9 is complete when:**
- The story can be finished from a new game, every story event has a guaranteed trigger, and a save that ignores the story still reaches every other system.
- The post-story dungeon can't be cleared in Dungeon 2 gear and can be cleared in its own crafted gear.
- Level-10 seeds, the crafting ceiling and New Game+ all work in a test save.
- A tester plays at least one more in-game season after the credits by choice, and story progress survives save → quit → load.

**Starts when:** [Phase 8](phase-08-town-life.md) is done.

**Steps:**
1. **Story outline.** A one-page arc: ~5 beats, a villain, an ending; each beat tied to an existing system (a dungeon, a festival, a villager); the rule at the top that the story never blocks farming or town life.
2. **Dungeon 3 and story boss.** Dungeon 3, ~20 minutes, on the Phase 7 framework; a story boss under the Phase 7 boss rules; after defeat, the boss joins the town as an NPC (schedule, tastes, lines).
3. **Story events and ending.** 4–5 story events in-engine (camera moves and Dialogic), each with a guaranteed trigger; an ending scene and credits.
4. **Post-story dungeon.** Unlocks after the credits, with the hardest monsters and best materials.
5. **Late-game crafting ceiling.** 2x and 8x multiplier materials (repeat the previous upgrade's effect) and one stat-inverting material (later penalties become bonuses), all found only in the post-story dungeon.
6. **Late-game farming and modes.** Level-10 seeds reachable through reaping within ~2 in-game years; contest scoring that rewards high crop levels; Hard difficulty; New Game+ after all board requests are done, with the carry-over decided and written here.
7. **Post-credits playtest.** A tester finishes the story and keeps playing; note what they do after the credits and why they stop.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Beating the story boss adds them as a villager the next day
- [ ] Every story event and the ending trigger in a test run from a new game with no random roll
- [ ] A weapon upgraded with an 8x material deals at least 3x *(default)* the damage of the same weapon without it
- [ ] The inverting material turns a later penalty into a bonus
- [ ] The multiplier and inverting materials drop only in the post-story dungeon
- [ ] A test save reaches level-10 seeds through reaping within 2 in-game years
- [ ] A higher-level crop scores higher in the contest
- [ ] Hard is selectable, and New Game+ starts with the carry-over written in this file

**Out of scope for this phase:** more arcs, anime cutscenes, children, magic.
